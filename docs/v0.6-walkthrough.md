# v0.6 walkthrough

v0.5 made drills trustworthy, but they were the same on every map: "learn
two pop-flashes for your site" left the player to find out which site and
which flash. v0.6 adds where on the map each mistake happens, so the coach
can say where to start.

## What changed

1. **Place names from the demo** ([ADR 15](decisions/0015-places.md)). CS2
   keeps a place name for every player ("LongA", "Banana", "BombsiteB"). The
   parser stores it for everyone alive at a kill and, for every flash,
   where it was thrown from; findings about a moment carry it. On the four test demos, every
   death had one.
2. **Counted per mistake, within a map.** History sums the places of each
   mistake per map, since "B site" exists on every map; briefs say
   "B site, Mirage". Coach input carries the top three per issue plus the
   place of every example. Names are turned into words: "BombsiteB" becomes
   "B site".
3. **Drills that say where.** The LLM names the spot where most findings
   happened; the rule-based coach adds "Start at Long A: 3 of your 5 were
   there" when a place holds at least two findings and a third of them.
4. **Utility guides.** Lineup drills now start with a community utility
   guide from the Workshop, drawn on the real map in practice mode, which is
   how players actually learn lineups.
5. **On the pages.** Evidence lines and the findings table show the place,
   drill cards list where the mistake happened most, and the progress page
   shows the top places under each mistake.

## Problems worth noting

- **No map data of our own.** Drawing site polygons for every map would be
  work to keep up to date with every map change. The game already names
  places, and players know those names.
- **What the review caught.** The first version added up places across
  maps, so "B site 6" could be two deaths each on three maps: a spot that
  does not exist. It also took a flash's place when it went off, by which
  time a player who pop-flashed had already moved. And the "a third of them"
  rule divided by every finding, including old ones without a place, which
  hid clear clusters. All three are fixed, with tests.
- **Old matches stay without places.** Demos are deleted after parsing, so
  matches analyzed before v0.6 cannot be filled in. The progress page's
  places build up with new matches.

## Tests

- Fixtures and golden reports rebuilt from the demos: the diff is only the
  new place fields, no counts changed.
- Place names in words; places summed across matches and sorted; the coach
  input of a real match carries places for its issues and examples; the
  rule-based coach names a place only when findings cluster there.
- A real Dust2 match through ChatGPT: the coach wrote "learn the two
  pop-flash lineups for your Outside Long approach".

## Known limits

- A place is an area, not a position: "A site" is big.

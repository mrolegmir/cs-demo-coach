# v0.8 walkthrough

v0.7 showed who trades whom. v0.8 adds what each player does for the
team: who takes the first fights, whose flashes actually blind enemies,
who plays the AWP. That turns "your entry keeps dying dry" into "Carl,
flash for Anna".

## What changed

1. **Roles in the report** ([ADR 17](decisions/0017-roles.md)). Per player
   and match: first duels taken and won, kills, AWP kills, flashes, and
   flashes that blinded an enemy for a second or more. Older reports are
   filled in from stored matches at api start, with the trade pairs.
2. **A role line on the progress page.** First fights against the lobby,
   first-fight win rate, blinding flashes per ten rounds against the
   lobby, and AWP share, with a label (entry, support, AWPer) only when
   the numbers are clearly above the lobby over 30 rounds or more.
3. **Role team advice.** The rule-based coach writes "Team: Carl, flash
   for Anna" when Anna is an entry whose dry first deaths stand out and
   Carl's flashes blind enemies most often, citing those deaths. The LLM
   gets the same numbers.

## Problems worth noting

- **Flash assists were too rare.** The first version counted kills on
  enemies a teammate's flash had blinded. On real matches that was 0 to 4
  per match for all ten players together, 0.1 per ten rounds: no label
  could ever show. Flashes that blind an enemy are about fifteen times as
  common and still measure useful utility, so the support role uses them.
- **No label is an answer.** Most players in a few matches have no clear
  role. The page says so and shows the numbers, rather than guessing.

## Tests

- Golden reports carry roles for every player; the backfill fills in
  roles and pairs for old reports and leaves findings alone.
- Labels: entry, support and AWPer each from their own numbers, and none
  below the round minimum.
- Role advice names the best flasher, cites the entry's first deaths in
  order, passes `Validate`, and is skipped when those deaths do not stand
  out.

## Known limits

- Roles mix both sides; entry mostly matters on T side.

# v0.7 walkthrough

The coach looked at players one by one; the team section only appeared when
several of them shared a mistake. Trading, the core team skill, depends on
the pair: a 20% trade rate can be one teammate who always trades you and
one who never does. v0.7 counts trades per pair of teammates.

## What changed

1. **Trade pairs** ([ADR 16](decisions/0016-trade-pairs.md)). For every
   death with a teammate within trading distance, the trade detector now
   records the pair (who died, who was nearest) and whether the killer was
   traded. A test checks pairs add up to the existing trade stats exactly.
2. **Backfill.** The api recomputes pairs once at start for reports made
   before v0.7, from the parsed match the database keeps.
3. **"Trading with teammates" on the progress page.** For each teammate
   with at least four deaths near each other: how often you are traded
   when you die near them, and how often you trade them.
4. **Team advice per pair.** Coach input carries the party's pairs (summed
   over the week for briefs). The LLM is told to use a pair that trades
   badly; the rule-based coach writes "Team: Bob, trade Anna" for the worst
   pair with at least four chances and a 25% trade rate or less, citing
   Bob's missed trades of Anna.

## Problems worth noting

- **Evidence still rules.** Pair advice is team advice, and team advice
  must cite findings. A pair that trades badly but has no missed trade to
  cite (the partner was out of trading distance) gives no advice; it shows
  as isolated deaths instead.
- **Small samples.** One match rarely gives four deaths near the same
  teammate, so pair advice mostly appears in weekly briefs.

- **What the review caught.** Briefs first summed pairs over all time
  instead of the week, and found evidence for pair advice only among the
  last few examples, so pair advice almost never appeared in briefs. A
  worker from before v0.7 would have saved reports without pairs until the
  next api restart. And the teammate list named people from matches the
  viewer was not in. All fixed: briefs use the week's matches and add the
  partner's missed trades as evidence, saving a detection fills in pairs,
  and the list shows only teammates the viewer may open.

## Tests

- Pairs add up to the trade stats on every fixture, and are always two
  teammates; recomputing them from a stored match gives the same pairs.
- The backfill finds only old reports and leaves findings alone; a report
  saved without pairs gets them.
- Brief pairs cover only the week and only party members, with the
  partner's missed trades as evidence.
- Partners are summed in both directions across matches.
- Pair advice picks the worst pair, skips thin samples, cites the right
  missed trades, and passes `Validate`.
- The progress page lists teammates with enough shared deaths.

## Known limits

- "Nearest teammate" is a straight line through walls, as for missed
  trades.

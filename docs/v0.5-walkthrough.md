# v0.5 walkthrough

Every focus area was already grounded in findings: the coach had to cite
rounds, and `Validate` rejected advice that did not. The drill was the one
part nobody checked. The LLM wrote it freely, so a habit got a different
drill each week, and the practice log could not tell what a player had
tried. v0.5 makes drills a curated library the coach has to pick from.

## What changed

1. **The drill library** ([ADR 14](decisions/0014-drill-library.md)).
   `internal/drills` holds two drills for each of the nine mistakes: steps,
   where they are done (offline server, your matches, with your party), how
   long, and a "done right" check. The offline ones share one practice
   server setup with real CS2 console commands.
2. **The coach picks, `Validate` checks.** Focus areas carry a `drill_id`.
   The LLM gets the drills for the kinds that stand out and must choose one;
   its `drill` text now says how it applies to this player's rounds.
   `Validate` rejects a missing or invented ID and a drill for another
   mistake, and sends the reason back to the model like any other grounding
   error.
3. **A different drill when one is not working.** If a player logged a
   drill for a mistake and the verdict is "no change yet", repeating that
   drill fails validation while another exists. The rule-based coach skips
   it too.
4. **Drill cards.** Match reports and briefs show the chosen drill's steps,
   time and check, the same wording everywhere, and a link that opens the
   practice form with the drill selected. A drill entry takes the drill's
   mistake, and its title and minutes when those are left empty.

## Problems worth noting

- **Grounding the drill, not just the reason.** The library is small on
  purpose: two good drills per mistake, reviewed once, beat a new invented
  one every week. The test that every mistake has at least two is what
  makes "pick another one" always possible.
- **Old advice.** Reports coached before v0.5 have no `drill_id`. They keep
  showing their free-text drill instead of failing to render.

## Tests

- Library: unique IDs, every mistake has two drills or more, offline drills
  have a time.
- `Validate`: no drill, an invented drill, a drill for another mistake, and
  a drill repeated after "no change yet" are rejected; the rule-based coach
  never repeats it.
- The LLM prompt carries the drills for the mistakes that stand out.
- Pages: the match report renders the drill card; the drill link
  preselects the drill; a drill entry is stored with the drill's mistake,
  title and time.
- A real match through ChatGPT (Codex provider): seven focus areas, all
  with valid drills.

## Known limits

- Two drills per mistake, none specific to a map yet; map lineups are the
  next step.
- Verdicts are per mistake, not per drill: there are too few matches a week
  to split them further.
